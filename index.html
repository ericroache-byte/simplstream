SimplStream

A simple internet radio tuner for iPhone. No banners, no pop-ups, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions.

  • Browse and search tens of thousands of internet radio stations by genre and country
  • Build and reorder your own favorites list
  • Add your own stations by stream URL
  • Keeps playing in the background while you use other apps

Support

Questions, problems, or a station that won't play? Email Mountaingent2000@yahoo.com and include the station name and, if possible, its stream URL.

Tip: if a station doesn't start, try another one. Some stations are only online part of the time or block certain regions.
