SimplStream — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

SimplStream ("the app") is a simple internet radio tuner. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Summary

  • We collect no personal data. No names, emails, phone numbers, locations, contacts, or device identifiers are collected by us.
  • No accounts. You never sign up or log in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

Your favorites list and any custom stations you add are stored only on your device. This data is never uploaded to us or anyone else. Deleting the app erases it.

Station directory

To list and search stations, the app requests data from the public Radio Browser directory (radio-browser.info). When you play a station from the directory, the app tells Radio Browser that the station was played (an anonymous play count that its API guidelines request). No personal information or identifier is attached.

Radio streams

Audio is streamed directly from each station's own server. As with any internet request, Radio Browser and the station servers receive standard request information such as your IP address and the time of the request. We do not control those third-party services; their handling of that information is governed by their own policies.

Children

The app is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same location with a new effective date.
